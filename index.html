The path from eligible to voted

Timelines depend on the election. Dates are announced by the Election Commission of India.

  1. Register

    File Form 6 on the Voters' Service Portal with proof of age, proof of address and a passport-size photograph.

  2. Confirm

    Search the electoral roll to confirm your name, and look up your assigned polling station before polling day.

  3. Prepare

    Carry your EPIC, or one of the alternative photo IDs notified by the Election Commission.

  4. Vote

    Get your identity checked, press the button on the ballot unit, and check the VVPAT slip shown behind the glass.
