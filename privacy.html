Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. This policy is available in English only.

VoteReady is an independent guide for first-time voters in India. It is built to work without collecting personal information. This page explains exactly what happens to data when you use it.

What we do not collect

  • We have no accounts, sign-ups or logins.
  • We do not ask for, receive or store your name, address, phone number, email address, EPIC number or any other voter identifier.
  • We do not record who or what you intend to vote for.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

What is stored on your device

VoteReady saves two small items in your browser's local storage so the site remembers your choices. They never leave your device through VoteReady, and you can clear them at any time by using "Reset progress" on the checklist or by clearing site data in your browser.

KeyPurpose
voteready_checklist_v1The list of checklist steps you have marked complete.
voteready_langYour language choice, English or Hindi.

The question and answer tool

When you submit a question, the text of the question, your language setting and the identifiers of the checklist steps you have completed are sent to our server. The server forwards the question and that context to Google's Gemini API to generate an answer, and returns the answer to you. We do not keep the question or the answer in a database of our own.

Google processes the request under its own terms and privacy policy for the Gemini API. Please do not type your name, voter ID, address or other personal details into the question box.

Server logs and abuse prevention

Our hosting provider, Vercel, processes technical request data such as IP address, time and requested page in ordinary server logs. We use your IP address, held temporarily in the server's memory, to limit how many questions can be asked in a short period so the service stays available for everyone. We do not use it to identify you.

State resources and directions

The state resources page loads a map of India from our own server. If you choose "Show directions from my location", your browser asks for permission to share your location. If you allow it, your browser sends your coordinates directly to Google Maps services to calculate a route. VoteReady does not receive or store your location, and nothing is requested from Google Maps until you press that button. If you choose "Open in Google Maps", the destination is passed to Google Maps in a new tab and Google handles your location under its own policy.

Third-party services

Loading our pages causes your browser to contact the following services, which may see your IP address and browser details:

  • Google Fonts, to load the typefaces used on the site.
  • Cloudflare's cdnjs, to load the D3 and TopoJSON libraries on the state resources page.
  • Google Maps Platform, only if you request directions.
  • Vercel, which hosts the site.

Links to the Election Commission of India and other official sites open those sites, which have their own privacy practices. VoteReady is not responsible for them.

Your choices and rights

Because VoteReady does not hold personal data about you, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe you have sent us personal information by mistake, contact us and we will help remove anything that can be identified. We aim to handle any personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

VoteReady is aimed at people who are about to vote or have recently become eligible. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
